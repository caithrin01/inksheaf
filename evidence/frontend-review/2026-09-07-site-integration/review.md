## Verdict

The direction landed. Across all seven captures there is one system: black masthead with the bold Inter-derived wordmark, warm off-white/paper grounds, native overhead 6×9 covers with no perspective tricks, and a consistent editorial rhythm of small caps eyebrow → large tight sans headline → quiet grey body. The covers read as plausible paperbacks rather than mockup props, and the four compositions are genuinely differentiated (masthead scale, centred literary, asymmetric band, italic) rather than four palettes of one layout. The author mark sits transparent on both light and dark grounds as approved, at charcoal and gold respectively, with no padded box visible. The print spread in image 7 is the strongest single asset — running heads, folios, subheads, justified measure — and it earns the "quiet page" claim visually.

The subscriber story is clear and correctly sequenced: hero states the promise, image 3 shows the mechanism (a real orange button inside a sample post), and the caption honestly scopes it as a sample announcement whose button opens an existing Lulu listing. "Readers order from the printer. Each copy is printed and mailed to them" removes the main ambiguity — who fulfils. The preview page reinforces it with per-copy cost, proof gating and reservation language, and no delivery promise.

## Visible defects

- **Image 4 gallery bleeds and clips.** Cards 01 and 04 are cut by the viewport, the "01" label sits with zero left margin, and the "See your publication in print" arrow is severed at the right edge. Whether this is a capture crop or a real container overflow, at this width it reads as broken framing on the section that carries the product's main choice.
- **Phone hero covers are too small to work.** At 390px the pair sits at roughly a third of the desktop scale with heavy overlap; "COLLECTED ESSAYS", the dates and the URL are sub-legible mush, and the tiny geometric mark loses its line weight. The desktop version's whole argument — these look like books — does not survive the reduction.
- **Preview panel repeats itself.** In image 5, 163 pages and $6.07 appear in the summary, the tab, the recommendation line, and again as "163pp $6.07"; the recommendation sentence is stated twice in near-identical wording. The panel reads like stacked partials rather than one edited column.

## Three improvements

1. Constrain the cover gallery to the page grid with equal outer margins, or make the overflow an intentional horizontal scroller with a visible affordance; ensure the section CTA is never truncated.
2. On phone, show one cover at near-full column width with a swipe or a small second thumbnail, so cover type stays readable.
3. Cut the duplicated price/page/recommendation lines in the preview to a single statement plus the tab row.

## Preference, not defect

Serif body in image 7 versus grey sans elsewhere is a deliberate register shift; I'd tighten it, but it isn't wrong.

## Not testable here

Motion, keyboard and focus behaviour, responsive reflow beyond these two widths, dark mode, real archive parsing, publication-identity correctness, and print fidelity cannot be judged from static captures. The 69-check pass and the browser matrix are your reports, not observations I can confirm.
