## Verdict on the direction

Not generic, but only just. The hero works: the outlined heavy lowercase at that scale, cropped tight against a near-black field, with the serif "Your writing has a life beyond the inbox." as counterpoint, is a real position — publisher voice in grotesk, book voice in serif. The earlier "grey + black grotesk" warning is answered mainly by the covers' colour, not by the guide's own pages, which are all the same off-white/black at the same rhythm (01, 04, 05, 07 have near-identical header构造: eyebrow left, two-line grotesk headline, right-column body). That is a preference-level flatness, not a blocker.

## Blockers

**1. Covers 02 and 04 are the same layout twice.** Both: centred title, short rule beneath, italic "2025 — 2026", centred dark logo plate, "caithrin.com" above a bottom rule. Only typeface and background differ. 01 and 03 are also close relatives (heavy lowercase title, flush left, date under). So the four read as two compositions × two palettes, not four points of view. Differentiate structurally: change title scale, axis, and where the metadata sits — e.g. give one a full-bleed type block with no top kicker, one a top-third title with a large empty lower field. Also: all four repeat "COLLECTED ESSAYS", "2025 — 2026", bottom rule and URL in the same places, which is what makes them feel templated. The shared safe area is right; the shared *ornament set* is not.

**2. The dark logo plate is unresolved on 03 and 04.** On 03 the black square sits high-left on pale green with nothing anchoring it — the only object above the olive block, reading as an accidental sticker. On 04 it is centred top and orphaned from the title. On 02 it lands as a dark blob directly under the date, competing with the title. The plate policy (2% padding, don't recolour) is correct and should stay; the *placement* needs a rule — e.g. plate always in the metadata zone at the foot, at one fixed size.

**3. Clear space defined as "the height of the i's dot" is too small** for a wordmark this heavy. On the reverse master the dot is a fraction of cap height; that rule permits crowding. Use the dot's *width* × 2, or x-height.

**4. Favicon evidence is missing, not wrong.** I accept 16/24/32px are the CSS sizes; the composite cannot show whether the square dot survives at 16px, where dot-to-stem gap is roughly a pixel. Add true 1:1 renders on both light and dark plates before this ships.

**5. Substack orange for a Lulu link.** Dark-on-#FF6719 looks plausibly AA but that is a measurement, not a screenshot claim — state the ratio. More important: styling a third-party commerce link in Substack's brand orange risks implying Substack fulfilment. The three-step list and the "does not create an order / preview link" note *do* explain the core feature clearly — that part is the guide's strongest copy. Add one line that this is the writer's own custom button, and show the button's disabled/preview state.

## Preferences

- The wordmark's distinctive assets are the square dot and the open **k**; the **f** terminal overhangs far right and leaves a visibly larger right sidebearing than the left of the **i** — worth tightening or noting as intentional. "ea" reads tighter than "sh".
- Tabletop contact is credible at this scale: shadow down/right, contact darkening at the base, consistent with the stated upper-left source. Two weaknesses: the shadow is uniformly soft with no tightening at the spine edge, and the double border on cover 02 makes it ambiguous whether the inner line is cover artwork or the object's edge. The enlarged side profile is too small to map to its "cover / paper block / back cover" caption — label the three bands directly.
