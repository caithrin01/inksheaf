**1. Volume change keeps the previous leaf index (high).** Image 3 shows Volume 2 with "Page 7 · leaf 10" and "Leaf 10 of 10" — the same coordinates as Volume 1's middle leaf, so a reader switching volumes lands somewhere arbitrary (here, coincidentally the last leaf) with no notice. Smallest fix: on volume change, reset to leaf 1 and re-render the Go to page list for that volume's true length.

**2. End-of-volume state is signalled only by grey "Next" text (high).** No border, cursor, or wording change; a creator cannot tell disabled from unstyled, and 28 total pages implies a second volume continues. Fix: mark it `aria-disabled` and add one line under the counter — "Last leaf of Volume 2 · 10 of 10" — plus a text link to Volume 1's first leaf if ordering allows.

**3. On phone, the whole download block precedes the reading tabs (medium).** Downloads occupy the first screen-and-a-half; "Pages" begins around 850px down, so in-page reading is the least discoverable route despite being the checkpoint's feature. Smallest fix: move the tab strip and viewer directly under the cover, downloads below; keep "Read your book" as an anchor to the tabs rather than a second, unexplained affordance.

**4. "Page 7 · leaf 10" is ambiguous about which number the control accepts (medium).** Two numbering systems are compressed into one option label, and the field label "Go to page" suggests printed numbering only. Fix: label options "Printed page 7 · physical leaf 10" and rename the control "Go to leaf"; keep the footer counter's two-line form, which reads correctly.

**5. Printed page / Read as text toggle has weak state and no visible grouping (medium).** Selected state is a pale fill only, the two controls sit flush with no separating gap on phone, and nothing indicates they are mutually exclusive. Fix: give the group a 1px outline, a clear selected fill/weight, `role="radiogroup"` with `aria-checked`, and 8px inner spacing so taps do not straddle.

**6. Printed-page canvas wastes most of its height (low).** The fixture's three lines sit top-left in a tall empty white area, so the viewer looks empty or mis-rendered even though it is correct. Fix: fit the canvas to the container width and cap displayed height, or add a caption "Full leaf shown at 6 × 9 inches" so the emptiness reads as paper, not failure. (Also check the "October 2, 2096" expiry string is fixture-only, not a hardcoded date.)

**What these captures cannot establish:** nothing here shows keyboard reachability or focus order across the selects, toggle and Previous/Next, nor whether arrow keys page the viewer; the toggle and volume/page changes cannot be proven to move focus or announce the new leaf to a screen reader. Motion, loading and error states of the PDF.js canvas, true responsive behaviour between these two widths, and whether the phone targets meet 44px in a live DOM are all unverifiable. Tap/hover and disabled contrast ratios were not measured. The screenshots cannot confirm that printed labels match the real PDF's internal numbering, that leaf counts (18 + 10 = 28) are derived rather than hardcoded, that downloads serve the correct volume, or that inbox delivery, link expiry, digest mismatch and changed-selection holds behave as described — those remain separately tested and outside this evidence.
